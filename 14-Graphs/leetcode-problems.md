## Practice Rules

- Understand vertices, edges, directed and undirected graphs.
- Learn adjacency lists and adjacency matrices.
- Practice BFS and DFS before advanced graph algorithms.
- Learn cycle detection and topological sorting.
- Study Dijkstra's algorithm before shortest-path challenges.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
