## Practice Rules

- Solve the problems in order.
- Attempt each problem before viewing the editorial.
- Write down the approach and time complexity.
- Test edge cases such as zero, negative numbers, and large integers.
- Avoid built-in shortcuts when the problem is designed to test the underlying algorithm.
- Revisit problems that require hints until you can solve them independently.
