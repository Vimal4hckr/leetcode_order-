## Practice Rules

- Solve problems in order.
- Practice character arrays and frequency maps.
- Understand the difference between a substring and a subsequence.
- Learn expand-around-center before advanced palindrome algorithms.
- Master fixed-size sliding windows before variable-size windows.
- Test empty strings, repeated characters, and case sensitivity.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
