## Practice Rules

- Trace loop variables for every iteration.
- Practice counters, accumulators, and nested loops.
- Identify repeated work that can be eliminated.
- Compare brute-force and optimized approaches.
- Test empty inputs, single elements, and boundary values.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
