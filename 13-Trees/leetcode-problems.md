## Practice Rules

- Understand root, child, leaf, and subtree.
- Draw the tree before solving each problem.
- Learn recursive traversal before iterative traversal.
- Practice preorder, inorder, postorder, and level-order traversal.
- Understand BST ordering properties.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
