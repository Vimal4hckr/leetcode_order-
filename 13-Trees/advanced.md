## Learning Objectives

After completing these problems, you should be able to:

- Reconstruct trees from traversal sequences.
- Serialize and deserialize tree structures.
- Find ancestors and relationships between arbitrary nodes.
- Apply dynamic programming and greedy strategies to trees.
- Solve tree path and subtree optimization problems.
- Analyze recursive algorithms and their time and space complexity.
