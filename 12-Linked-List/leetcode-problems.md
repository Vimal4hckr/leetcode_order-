## Practice Rules

- Understand nodes, values, and next pointers.
- Draw linked lists before changing their connections.
- Practice traversal before reversal.
- Learn dummy nodes to simplify insertion and deletion.
- Understand fast and slow pointers.
- Test empty lists, single-node lists, and cycles.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
