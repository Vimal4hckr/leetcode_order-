## Learning Objectives

After completing these problems, you should be able to:

- Reverse linked lists iteratively and recursively.
- Use fast and slow pointers to detect cycles and locate middle nodes.
- Merge and sort linked lists efficiently.
- Insert, delete, and rearrange nodes without losing references.
- Analyze linked-list algorithms in terms of time and auxiliary space complexity.
