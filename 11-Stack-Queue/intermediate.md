## Learning Objectives

After completing these problems, you should be able to:

- Implement stacks, queues, circular queues, and deques.
- Use stacks to evaluate expressions and simplify paths.
- Apply monotonic stacks to next-greater-element and range problems.
- Use queues and deques for sliding-window algorithms.
- Analyze time and space complexity for each solution.
