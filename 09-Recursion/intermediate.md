## Practice Rules

- Identify the base case before writing recursive calls.
- Trace the call stack for small inputs.
- Understand what each recursive call returns.
- Learn divide and conquer before advanced backtracking.
- Avoid unnecessary global variables.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
