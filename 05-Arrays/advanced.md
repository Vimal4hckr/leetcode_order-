## Practice Rules

- Attempt a brute-force solution first.
- Learn prefix sums and sliding windows before monotonic structures.
- Understand binary search on the answer where applicable.
- Test duplicates, negative values, and boundary cases.
- Explain the optimization and prove why it works.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
