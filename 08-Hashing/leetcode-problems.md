## Practice Rules

- Solve the problems in order.
- Understand sets, maps, keys, and values.
- Practice frequency counting before using advanced techniques.
- Compare brute-force solutions with hash-based solutions.
- Test duplicates, negative values, and empty inputs where applicable.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
