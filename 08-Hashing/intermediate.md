## Practice Rules

- Understand hash maps and hash sets before solving advanced problems.
- Practice frequency counting and grouping.
- Learn prefix sums before subarray-sum problems.
- Handle duplicate keys and repeated values carefully.
- Compare brute-force and hash-based approaches.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
