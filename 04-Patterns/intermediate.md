## Practice Rules

- Trace row and column indices carefully.
- Practice nested loops before attempting spiral traversal.
- Understand matrix boundaries and direction changes.
- Test rectangular matrices as well as square matrices.
- Avoid modifying the input unless the problem permits it.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
