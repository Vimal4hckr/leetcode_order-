## Practice Rules

- Start with left and right pointers.
- Understand when to move each pointer.
- Trace pointer positions after every iteration.
- Practice sorted-array problems before pair and triplet problems.
- Compare brute-force and optimized approaches.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
