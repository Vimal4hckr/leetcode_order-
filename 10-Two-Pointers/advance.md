## Learning Objectives

After completing these problems, you should be able to:

- Identify when two pointers can optimize array and string algorithms.
- Combine two pointers with greedy strategies and sliding windows.
- Process sorted arrays and intervals efficiently.
- Understand when hashing, sorting, binary search, or other techniques are needed alongside two pointers.
- Analyze time and space complexity and explain trade-offs.
