## Learning Objectives

After completing these problems, you should be able to:

- Apply left and right pointers to sorted arrays.
- Find pairs and triplets that satisfy a condition.
- Remove duplicates and modify arrays in place.
- Compare strings efficiently using pointers.
- Recognize when two pointers can reduce time complexity.
- Explain the time and space complexity of each solution.
