## Learning Objectives

After completing these problems, you should be able to:

- Identify overlapping subproblems and optimal substructure.
- Implement memoization and bottom-up tabulation.
- Define DP states and recurrence relations.
- Optimize space when previous states are sufficient.
- Apply DP to sequences, strings, grids, and subset problems.
- Analyze time and space complexity.
