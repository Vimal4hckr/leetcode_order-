## Learning Objectives

After completing these problems, you should be able to:

- Design multidimensional DP states and transitions.
- Apply interval DP to partitioning and range-based optimization.
- Solve advanced string matching and subsequence problems.
- Optimize stock trading and scheduling decisions.
- Recognize when memoization, tabulation, or state compression is appropriate.
- Analyze the time and space complexity of advanced DP algorithms.
